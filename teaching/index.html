Teaching

I teach computer science and cybersecurity at Cardozo Education Campus, a public school in Washington, DC, and serve as District Computer Science Chair for DC Public Schools.

Classroom

I teach about 100 students a year in three courses: AP Computer Science Principles, PLTW Cybersecurity, and Computer Science Essentials.

  • Browser-only by design. Labs run on a school Chromebook behind a content filter, with no installs, accounts, or API keys.
  • Security inside other subjects. Micro:bit radios teach the CIA triad in physics, Caesar ciphers teach modular arithmetic, and password entropy teaches logarithms.
  • Language support. Materials include Spanish supports for multilingual learners.
  • Real practitioners. Guest speakers from Amazon, Google, and policy think tanks connect class to careers.
